In this game, players compete to assemble a valid
graph of stars as fast as possible. Each star 
has either color red or color blue. The assembled
graph must be bipartite (two-colored). Thus, blue
stars can only connect to red stars and vice versa. 
Furthermore, each star has a maximum degree. Once 
the each star obtains its maximum degree, 
the assembly is finished, and the player's time is
recorded. Once all players are finished, their ranking is calculated in ascending order of finishing time. The game can be played ranging from 2 to 4 players. 

Input is done by moving a virtual cursor on the screen using a gamepad joystick and pressing A to select and unselect stars. 

Once all the stars are fully connected for a player, they are animated to move to pre-determined destination spots to form a constellation.
